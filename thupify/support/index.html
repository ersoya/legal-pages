About Thupify

Thupify is a private, local-first decision assistant that helps you compare options against what matters to you and reach a clearer decision on your iPhone.

Local Data

Your decisions are stored on your device and are not synced in this version. You can delete individual decisions or all local decision data from the app.

Purchases

You can manage or cancel your subscription from your App Store account settings. To recover a previous purchase, use Restore Purchase in Thupify's settings.
