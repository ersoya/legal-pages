Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Thupify ("we", "our", or "us") is designed as a private, local-first decision assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Handle

Thupify does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address to use the core decision features.

The following categories of information may be handled:

  • Decision content you enter, including questions, options, criteria, notes, priorities, scores, results, and decision history
  • Limited app interaction data, such as feature usage, flow steps, option and criterion counts, result categories, and subscription status
  • Technical and diagnostic information, such as app version, device and operating system details, crash reports, and performance-related logs
  • Purchase and subscription information needed to provide Thupify Premium

2. Local Decision Data

Your decision questions, options, criteria, notes, scores, results, and history are stored locally on your device. This decision content is not synced to our servers and is not included in our analytics events.

You can delete individual decisions or all locally stored decision data from within the app. Deleting the app may also remove its locally stored data, subject to your device and backup settings.

If you choose to share a result card, the content is passed through Apple's system share sheet to the person, app, or service you select. Information you share is then handled according to that recipient's or service's privacy practices.

3. On-Device Recommendations

Thupify calculates recommendations on your device using the options, criteria, priorities, and scores you provide. Your decision content is not sent to an external artificial intelligence or decision-processing service.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

We use limited usage events and crash diagnostics to understand how the app is used, maintain reliability, and improve features. These events are designed not to include the text of your questions, options, criteria, or notes.

We do not use your decision content for advertising, and Thupify does not use it to track you across apps or websites.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple, and subscription access is managed with RevenueCat. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, product identifiers, transaction information, and an app-specific identifier as necessary to complete purchases and maintain your Premium access.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

6. How We Use Information

Limited technical, usage, diagnostic, and purchase-related information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain app features
  • Apply free-tier limits and remotely configured app settings
  • Understand feature usage and improve the app experience
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes and technical issues
  • Process purchases, restore subscriptions, and confirm Premium access

7. Third-Party Services

Thupify uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics – to understand aggregate app usage and engagement
  • Firebase Crashlytics – to collect crash reports and diagnose technical issues
  • Firebase Remote Config – to provide remotely configured limits and app settings
  • RevenueCat – to manage purchases, subscriptions, and Premium entitlement status
  • Apple App Store – to process payments and manage subscriptions

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and only for the functionality they provide to Thupify.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or decision content. Limited information may be processed by the service providers listed above, as necessary to provide their functionality, or disclosed when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Your decision data remains subject to the security of your device, operating system, passcode, backups, and any other device-level protections you use. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

10. Children's Privacy

Thupify is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
