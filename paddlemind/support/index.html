About PaddleMind

PaddleMind is a private pickleball coaching app that analyzes one short shot video and gives you one clear priority to work on next.

Recording a Good Clip

  • Use a 5–15 second video containing one target shot.
  • Keep one primary player, the full body, and the paddle visible.
  • Place the phone on a stable surface and record in usable lighting.
  • If analysis confidence is low, follow the recapture guidance shown in the app.

Video Privacy

V1 shot analysis runs on your device. Videos and extracted frames are not uploaded for analysis or model training, and an imported Photo Library item is never modified or deleted by PaddleMind.
