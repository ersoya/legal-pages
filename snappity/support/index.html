About Snappity

Snappity lets you photograph a receipt, extract items automatically, assign them to people, and calculate each person's share of the bill.

Getting a Good Scan

  • Lay the receipt flat on a contrasting, well-lit surface.
  • Keep the full receipt in frame, avoiding creases, glare, and shadows.
  • Hold the camera steady and directly above the receipt.
  • If items are missing or incorrect, you can review and edit them before splitting.

Splitting a Bill

After scanning, assign each item to one or more people in the Split screen. Unassigned items are split evenly across everyone on the bill. The Summary screen shows each person's total, which you can save or share.

Receipt Privacy

Receipt scanning runs on your device. Your receipt photos and extracted item data are not uploaded for processing or model training.
