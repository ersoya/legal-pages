Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Snappity ("we", "our", or "us") is designed as a private, local-first receipt-splitting app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Handle

Snappity does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address to use its core features.

The following categories of information may be handled:

  • Photos of receipts you capture for scanning
  • Text and item data extracted from receipts via on-device optical character recognition (OCR)
  • People, item assignments, and per-person totals you create when splitting a bill
  • Saved receipts, items, and people stored locally on your device
  • Limited app interaction and operational data
  • Technical and diagnostic information, such as app version, device and operating system details, and crash reports
  • Purchase and subscription information needed to provide Snappity's paid features

2. Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used only to photograph a receipt for scanning. If you select a receipt image from your Photo Library instead, that access is used only to import the image, and the original item in your Photo Library is not modified or deleted.

3. On-Device Receipt Scanning

Receipt image capture, text recognition, and item extraction run on your device. Your receipt photos and the text extracted from them are not uploaded for processing or used for model training, and Snappity does not send your receipt images to an external artificial intelligence service.

4. Local Storage and Retention

  • Receipts, items, and people you create are stored locally on your device using Core Data until you delete them or remove the app.
  • Saved receipts remain in the app until you delete them individually or remove the app.
  • Receipt photos used for scanning are processed on-device and are not retained beyond what is needed to complete the scan, unless you choose to save the receipt.

Removing Snappity deletes data in the app container, subject to your device and backup settings.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

We use limited operational events to understand app reliability and improve the app experience. These events may include:

  • When a receipt is scanned, saved, deleted, or shared
  • When a bill split is calculated
  • Crash reports and normalized failure information

Operational events do not include receipt images, extracted item text, item prices, or the names of people you add to a split.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple, and subscription access is managed with RevenueCat. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, product identifiers, transaction information, and an app-specific identifier as necessary to complete purchases and maintain your access to Snappity's paid features.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

7. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Scan receipts, extract items, and calculate per-person totals
  • Save and display your receipt history
  • Let you share a bill summary with others
  • Understand feature usage and improve reliability
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes and technical issues
  • Process purchases, restore subscriptions, and confirm access to paid features

8. Third-Party Services

Snappity uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics – to understand aggregate app usage and engagement
  • Firebase Crashlytics – to collect crash reports and sanitized technical failures
  • RevenueCat – to manage purchases, subscriptions, and entitlement status
  • Apple App Store – to process payments and manage subscriptions

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and only for the functionality they provide to Snappity.

9. Data Sharing and Tracking

We do not sell your personal information, receipt data, or bill history. Snappity does not use your data for cross-app advertising tracking. Limited information may be processed by the service providers listed above as necessary to provide their functionality, or disclosed when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Local data remains subject to the security of your device, operating system, passcode, backups, and any other device-level protections you use. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

11. Children's Privacy

Snappity is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
